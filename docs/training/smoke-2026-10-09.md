| PunctCase | 39,434 | 437.52 s (7.29 min) | 431.87 s (7.20 min) | 407.69 MiB |

Outer wall includes startup and corpus/prepared hash guards. RSS is Linux `RUSAGE_CHILDREN.ru_maxrss`: the largest waited child/descendant high-water mark, not a summed process-tree or host peak. The 32 GiB ceiling is a configured limit, not measured usage.

CharSpell selected epoch **2** of 2 completed epochs by validation loss. Aggregate top-1: 93,777/95,735 (97.95%); top-3: 99.53%. The always-retain-input baseline already achieves 95.08% on those same emitted word examples, so aggregate accuracy alone is misleading.

Changed-only top-1, conditional on retrieved target: 3,056/4,707 (64.92%). Counting 333 retrieval misses as failures gives 3,056/5,040 (60.63%). Unchanged-word false corrections: 307/91,028 (0.34%). Unsupported/unqueried errors remain outside both changed-word denominators.

PunctCase selected epoch **2** of 2. Punctuation micro-F1: 89.97%; punctuation accuracy: 93,714/96,410 (97.20%). Case accuracy: 105,422/105,652 (99.78%). Punctuation classes are NONE, comma, period, question mark, exclamation mark and colon; case classes are lower/title/upper/mixed. Micro-F1 excludes NONE as a positive; accuracy can be dominated by frequent classes. Protected/ambiguous labels are excluded and no class-balanced or human-reviewed quality is measured.

WordLM fitted 100,000 clean sentences / 1,802,864 tokens including EOS. Validation perplexity: **456.8073**, with 0 zero-probability tokens among 143,391 predictions. This measures mapped vocabulary/suffix classes including EOS, not unrestricted surface-word perplexity or spelling-correction benefit. Suffix classes are heuristic rather than certified morphology.
