# Review of 201 mismatches and expansion to 1,000 cases

Source main: 226da56bafc7ae3fd9d00cd7e36af78c2082c785.

The previous corpus had 599/800 exact matches. Every remaining input/output
pair was examined. Engine fixes address missing diacritics and mixed-script
spellings, false correction of valid words, foreign term suffixes, English
capitalization, relative versus direct questions, conditional/concessive
commas, genitive/participle false boundaries and completed reports followed
by requests. Shared processing applies the same repairs to text and mail.
There are no full-input replacements and no runtime evaluation-fixture imports.

The exact editorial targets themselves needed six corrections. Each old/new
value and its linguistic reason is recorded in
`independent-gold-v2.json` under `review.targetRevisions20260930`. The original
800 input hashes and baseline protected IDs remain unchanged. Target hashes
were explicitly updated for that review, not regenerated from engine output.

Added 100 text cases and 100 mail cases covering separate document actions and
report/request combinations. These are compositional synthetic examples built
from authored subjects/actions/reports/requests. Their expected outputs are
specified before evaluation. They are not 200 independently collected natural
texts, nor blind human-certified tests. The source generator is reproducible.
All 1,000 targets are evaluation-only and excluded from model training.

## Results

| Scope | Exact outputs |
|---|---:|
| Original RSD/IT | 460/460 |
| Original mail | 340/340 |
| New text | 100/100 |
| New mail | 100/100 |
| Second-pass stability | 1,000/1,000 |
| Separate 30 fresh probes | 30/30 text and mail body |
| Separate noisy synthetic test pairs | 57/200 (rules baseline 45/200) |
| Separate noisy synthetic validation pairs | 21/100 (rules baseline 19/100) |

CI now requires `gold:exact`, verifies target/input hashes and regenerates the
200 added cases and local model without artifact differences. The regression
suite passes all 3,765 tests, including these 1,000 comparisons rather than
just checking vocabulary anchors. Local typecheck, lint, build, fresh evaluations and latency benchmarks
are also run.

The engine is still a conservative rule/morphology/statistical hybrid. These
results establish conformance to this reviewed corpus; they do not establish
general semantic understanding or guaranteed correction of arbitrary prose.
The separate noisy-corruption corpus above shows that broader spelling and
phrasing coverage still needs work.
