The upstream 148-sentence grammatical-example treebank includes North/South
variety differences. We use token/POS/lemma annotations as linguistic examples,
not as authoritative standard-Azerbaijani spelling targets. Sentence-family
splits keep alternates and normalized duplicate texts together. It is small
and cannot establish general POS accuracy. Derived POS artifact is also
CC BY-SA 4.0. No upstream data is downloaded during application requests.
